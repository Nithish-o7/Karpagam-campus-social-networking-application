# BRAND GUIDELINES

# KCE Connect Brand Guidelines

## Brand Name
**KCE Connect**

May also be referred to contextually as the "Karpagam Campus App". 

### Acceptable Usage
- KCE Connect
- KCE CONNECT (all caps)

### Unacceptable Usage
- Kce Connect
- KCEConnect (no space)
- Karpagam Connect (unless used as a subtitle)

---

## Color Palette

### Primary Colors

**Karpagam Crimson** (Standard Academic Primary)
- HEX: #A6192E
- RGB: 166, 25, 46
- Use as: Primary brand color, headers, primary buttons, alert tags

**Campus Navy**
- HEX: #1A2254
- RGB: 26, 34, 84
- Use as: Secondary branding, deep backgrounds, footer, active tab states

### Secondary Colors

**Innovation Blue**
- HEX: #4A5FD9
- Use as: Accents, links, "In Progress" ticket state badges

**Success Green**
- HEX: #28A745
- Use as: Success states, "Resolved" ticket badges, SLA met indicators

**Warning Gold**
- HEX: #FFC107
- Use as: Pending states, SLA warning indicators

### Neutral Colors

**White**
- HEX: #FFFFFF
- Use as: App background, feed card backgrounds

**Campus Gray**
- HEX: #F0F2F5
- Use as: Feed background (to make white post cards pop), subtle dividers

**Text Dark**
- HEX: #1A1A1A
- Use as: Headings, primary post text

---

## Typography

### Primary Font
**Inter** (Clean, modern, and highly readable on mobile devices)

### Font Weights
- **Bold (700)**: Names on posts, Section Headings
- **Semi-Bold (600)**: Subheadings, Buttons, Ticket IDs
- **Regular (400)**: Post content, descriptions, comments
- **Light (300)**: Timestamps, secondary metadata

---

## UI Elements

### Buttons
**Primary Action Button (e.g., Submit Ticket, Post)**
- Background: Karpagam Crimson (#A6192E)
- Text: White (#FFFFFF)
- Border Radius: 24px (Pill shape for modern social feel)
- Hover/Tap: Darken 10%

**Secondary Button (e.g., Cancel, Outline)**
- Background: Transparent
- Border: 2px solid Campus Navy (#1A2254)
- Text: Campus Navy (#1A2254)
- Border Radius: 24px

### Cards (Social Posts & Tickets)
- Background: White (#FFFFFF)
- Border Radius: 12px
- Border: 1px solid #EAEAEA (No heavy drop shadows, keep it flat and clean)

### Status Badges (Crucial for Tickets)
- Shape: Small pill shape with 12px text
- Padding: 4px 8px
- Resolved: Success Green background / White text
- In Progress: Innovation Blue background / White text
- New/Pending: Warning Gold background / Dark text

---

## Tone of Voice
- **Community-Driven**: Encouraging, inclusive, and collegiate.
- **Efficient**: When dealing with the "Report Issue" flow, language should be concise and action-oriented.
- **Professional**: Maintains the respect of an academic environment while being accessible to students.
